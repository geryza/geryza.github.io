Homelabs, self-hosting, and everything that breaks in them.

The channel

Geryza's Universe is a YouTube channel about self-hosting and homelab infrastructure, taught from a real lab's real failures — what broke, what the metrics actually said, and what we changed.

It is narrated by Geryza. How it is made: each script is drafted by an AI model (Claude) from my own lab notes and reviewed line by line by me; the voice is synthetic (Kokoro TTS); every picture, the music and the sound effects are generated by code. No real person's voice or face is used.

The channel's publishing tool

The channel is published by a small program I run on my own computer. It signs in to the channel's own Google account and uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the channel's videos, with their title, description, chapters, captions, thumbnail and the AI-content disclosure;
  • read each video back to check it went up exactly as it was approved;
  • read the channel's own analytics and reports — views, watch time, audience retention, impressions and click-through rate — to learn what works;
  • read the comments on the channel's videos, and post the channel's own comment under a new video.

It works only on this channel, for its owner. It is not offered to anyone else, it sells and shares nothing, and it shows no ads. What it reads and keeps is described in the Privacy Policy; the rules for using it are in the Terms of Service.
